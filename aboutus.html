Our Community

Barangay Sauyo is more than just a residential area; it is a thriving ecosystem of green thumbs and community builders. Through the "GrowSauYOU" initiative, we have transformed idle spaces into productive urban farms, fostering food security and environmental awareness among our neighbors.

Our mission is simple: Grow together. By integrating technology (I-Tanim), knowledge sharing (Green Community), and resource sharing (Here-Ramin), we are building a self-sufficient and resilient barangay for the future.

Our Urban Farm

Located in the heart of Sauyo, our community urban farm serves as the hub for all agricultural activities. Here, volunteers learn sustainable farming techniques, grow organic vegetables, and harvest produce that is distributed to local families and soup kitchens.

  • Composting & Waste Management
  • Hydroponics Systems
  • Community Seed Library
  • Weekly Harvest Markets
